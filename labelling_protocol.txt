General ruling
- If there are multiple suitable categories, the theme most frequently mentioned is chosen as the category
- If there are ties, prioritise from top to bottom of the list
- If there are no suitable categories or ambiguous narratives, only focus on keywords, and follow the same rules as above as you see fit

Credit card
- Any case involving a Credit Card
- Card can be physical or digital, as long a card is involved
- Any purchase issues
- Does not involve credit card debt

Credit reporting
- Any case involving records of your credit payment history (compiled as a report)
- Lenders use it to evaluate creditworthiness to consider loans or credit approval
- Affects recording and reporting of credit transaction record

Mortgage
- Any case involving property
- Takes priority even if loan, debt, and money is involved

Consumer loan 
- Any case involving Loans & Loaning Money

Debt collection
- Any case involving debt and repaying via collection
- Any case involving a debt collecting company
- Cases where the case continues even if the debt is settled

Bank account or service
- Any case involving a Bank or Account
- Any bank or account counts towards this

Money transfer or service
- Any case involving Money or Money Services
- Lowest priority since there are more niche Money cases above
